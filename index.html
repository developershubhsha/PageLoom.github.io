Common questions

How do I import a PDF?
Tap Add PDFs and choose files from the Files app, or open a PDF in PageLoom from another app.

What does the free version include?
You can merge, split, reorder, rotate, compress, and password-protect PDFs with up to 10 pages in a workspace.

What does the one-time purchase unlock?
Unlimited pages in a workspace. There is no subscription. Use Restore Purchase if you already bought it on this Apple ID.

Do my documents leave this iPhone?
No. PageLoom processes files on the device. It does not create an account or upload PDFs.

Why did compression change my PDF?
Compression turns every page into an image so the file gets smaller. Searchable text, links, forms, and annotations are removed. The app warns you before export.

I bought Unlimited but still see the 10-page limit.
Open PageLoom Pro and tap Restore Purchase while signed into the same Apple ID you used to buy.

Is PageLoom on iPad or Mac?
The first release is iPhone only.

What PageLoom does not do

It does not scan paper, run OCR, add signatures, or convert Word or Excel files.
