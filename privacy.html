Privacy Policy

Last updated 4 October 2026.

Support Privacy

PageLoom is built so your documents stay on your iPhone. The app does not create an account, does not upload PDFs, and does not include advertising or analytics SDKs.

Data PageLoom does not collect

  • PDF contents, filenames, or passwords
  • Name, email, or an in-app account
  • Location, contacts, photos, or microphone access
  • Usage analytics or crash reports sent to the developer

What stays on your device

Imported PDFs, page thumbnails, export files, and the appearance setting are stored only on this iPhone. Temporary working files are deleted when you dismiss a share sheet, leave the app, clear the workspace, or next open PageLoom.

Purchases

The optional Unlimited unlock is a one-time In-App Purchase through Apple. Apple processes payment and can see your Apple ID and transaction data under Apple’s privacy policy. PageLoom only receives a signed receipt that you own the unlock. It does not receive your card details.

Permissions

PageLoom reads PDFs you choose in the Files picker or open into the app. It does not need camera, photos, or tracking permission.

Children

PageLoom does not collect personal data and is not directed at children. The App Store age rating is 4+.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page will change. Continued use after an update means you accept the new policy.
